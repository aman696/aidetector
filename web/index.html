How it works

1

You drop an image

It's sent over an encrypted connection and held only in temporary memory for the scan.

2

We read the clues

The scanner inspects texture, noise, sharpness, colour and frequency patterns that differ between real cameras and AI generators.

3

You get a verdict

A clear estimate with a confidence level and the plain-language signals behind it. Then your scan is deleted — or you can contribute it, with your label, to help the detector improve.

Privacy & helping out

There's no account and no sign-up. A normal scan runs in temporary storage for a few seconds and is then deleted — we don't keep it. If you'd like to help, you can contribute an image together with your own real-or-AI label: contributed images are stored and used to improve the detector, and every one makes it better on real-world cases. Only contribute images you're comfortable sharing.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes — free, with no account or sign-up.
Do you keep my photos?
Only if you choose to. A normal scan is deleted the moment it finishes. If you tap a "contribute" button after a result, that image and your label are stored to help improve the detector.
How accurate is it?
It reliably flags mainstream AI generators and real photos, and holds up on social-media and screenshot images. It's an estimate, not proof — newer "flow-style" generators are harder, so always use your own judgment too.
What can it detect?
Fully AI-generated images from common tools (Stable Diffusion, DALL·E, Midjourney, Firefly, and chat-AI image makers). It is not a deepfake/face-swap or photo-edit detector.
